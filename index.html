How FINOVA Works

The definitive architecture for autonomous capital liquidity.

1

Invoice Cryptography

Suppliers upload invoices which are instantly hashed via SHA-256, creating an immutable cryptographic signature on the ledger. Fraud becomes mathematically impossible.

2

AI Matching Engine

Our sophisticated autonomous agents analyze risk, liquidity pools, and historical performance to instantly bid on verified invoices.

3

Instant Settlement

Once matched, capital is deployed directly to the supplier's treasury within milliseconds, enabling seamless operational continuity.
